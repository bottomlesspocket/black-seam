Black Seam: style mockups

Wales, 1871. Elias Crane buys up the mineral rights under a valley, one farm at a time. Below are three art styles for the same scene, plus the intrigue mechanic.

1. Painterly 2.5D

The closest to the film's look: dusty ochre light, black silhouettes, industry dwarfing one man.

2. Modern pixel art

The cheapest and fastest to build. The tycoon's methods become verbs: "Bribe" and "Lie to".

3. Penny-newspaper engraving

The most distinctive look. The Gazette prints the official story, and you click through it to find out what really happened.

4. The intrigue mechanic: a leverage ledger

This replaces the inventory. You collect what people want and what they're hiding, and keep track of your own secrets.
